# SOP-02 Hazardous material storage

SOP SOP-02: Hazardous material storage. The procedure has 9 steps. Materials must be kept between -5°C and 3°C throughout. Deviations are escalated to the Compliance Officer within 2 hours. Governing policy: [[POL-02]].
