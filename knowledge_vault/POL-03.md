# POL-03 Industrial Adhesives returns and approvals

Policy POL-03 governs Industrial Adhesives returns and approvals. Customers may return Industrial Adhesives within 60 days of delivery. Any single order above $1,000 requires written approval from the Operations Director. Return records are retained for 10 years. Handling follows [[SOP-03]].
